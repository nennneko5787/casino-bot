DejaVuSans.ttf bundled in this directory is used to render chess piece
glyphs (U+265A-U+265F), which the IPAex font does not contain.

DejaVu Sans originates from the DejaVu fonts project, derived from
Bitstream Vera Sans under the Bitstream Vera Fonts Copyright, with
additional glyphs under the Arev Fonts Copyright. Both licenses are
permissive: the font may be freely used, copied, merged, modified and
redistributed (including bundled with software), provided the copyright
and trademark notices are preserved. See:
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

Source of this copy: matplotlib's bundled fonts
(.venv/Lib/site-packages/matplotlib/mpl-data/fonts/ttf/DejaVuSans.ttf).
